Privacy Policy

Last updated: MAY 2026

1. General information

This application is an independently developed video game. It does not require user registration, account creation, or the submission of personal data.

The developer is committed to respecting user privacy and complying with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Locally stored data

The application may store information locally on the user's device to preserve continuity between sessions.

This data may include:

  • Game scores and play times
  • Unlocked achievements and medals
  • Unlocked cosmetic items
  • Game settings and preferences

This data remains stored exclusively on the user's device.

3. Online features and global leaderboard

The application includes optional online features related to a global leaderboard.

If the user chooses to participate in the leaderboard, the following data may be shared:

  • Scores or play times
  • A nickname chosen by the user
  • An anonymous device identifier

This data is used exclusively for the operation of the global leaderboard and is not used to personally identify users.

4. Content sharing

The application includes an optional feature that allows users to share scores through external applications such as WhatsApp.

This action is voluntarily initiated by the user and does not grant the developer access to conversations, contacts, or shared content.

5. Advertising and monetization

The application does not currently display advertisements or include in-app purchases.

If these features are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Children

This application is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

The developer reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be published on this page.
