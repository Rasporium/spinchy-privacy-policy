Privacy Policy

Last updated: NOVEMBER 2026

1. General information

Spinchy is an independently developed video game created by Rasporium. Users are not required to register, provide an email address, or create an account using a username and password.

The game includes optional online features that allow players to participate in leaderboards and store certain progression data associated with an anonymous player profile.

The developer responsible for Spinchy is Antonio Miguel Fernández Perales, publishing the application under the name Rasporium.

The developer is committed to respecting user privacy and complying with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Locally stored data

The application may store information locally on the user's device to preserve continuity between sessions.

This data may include:

  • Game scores and play times
  • Accumulated points
  • Unlocked medals and achievements
  • Unlocked outfits and cosmetic items
  • Game settings and preferences

This data remains stored on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers, except when using the online features described in this policy.

3. Online features and player profiles

Spinchy uses LootLocker, an external backend service provided by LootLocker AB, to manage the game's online features.

To use these features, the game may automatically generate a player profile through a guest login system (Guest Login). This process does not require users to provide their real name, email address, or password.

LootLocker generates a unique player identifier that allows the profile to be recognized in future sessions.

Information associated with this profile may include:

  • A unique player identifier generated by LootLocker
  • A nickname chosen by the user
  • Scores and play times achieved in different game modes
  • Information about rewards, medals, and achievements obtained through leaderboards
  • Session data required for online functionality

This information is used exclusively to provide the game's online features, such as identifying players on leaderboards, storing their progress, and managing earned rewards.

4. Leaderboards and scores

The game includes different online leaderboards, both global and weekly, for its various difficulty modes.

Scores are associated with the corresponding player profile to allow players to be identified and ranked on the leaderboards.

Weekly leaderboards reset periodically. When a reset occurs, scores are removed from the active weekly leaderboard. Historical records may be retained through the service's archiving systems.

5. Player rewards and assets

Spinchy uses LootLocker's asset system to store certain rewards associated with player profiles.

This information may include medals and rewards obtained through leaderboard placements in the weekly leaderboards for the different difficulty modes.

This allows players to retain their achievement history and access their earned rewards across sessions.

6. Third-party service providers and data processing

LootLocker AB acts as a service provider and data processor for the player data required to provide the game's online features.

You can consult its own Privacy Policy at:

https://lootlocker.com/privacy-policy/

The developer does not use this data for advertising, commercial profiling, or personal identification of players.

Data is used exclusively for the purposes described in this policy.

7. Content sharing

The application includes an optional feature that allows users to share scores through external applications such as WhatsApp.

This action is voluntarily initiated by the user. The developer does not access users' conversations, contacts, or private communications.

8. Advertising and monetization

Spinchy does not currently display advertisements or include in-app purchases.

If these features are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears the application's data, or uninstalls the game.

Data associated with player profiles is stored in LootLocker for as long as necessary to provide online features and maintain player progression and earned rewards.

Weekly leaderboard scores are no longer active after the corresponding reset. Historical records may be retained according to the service's archiving functionality.

Users may request information about, correction of, or deletion of data associated with their player profile by contacting the developer using the email address provided in this policy.

10. Children

Spinchy is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly request personal information from minors.

11. User rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, users may exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and objection, where applicable.

To submit a request concerning their data, users may contact the developer using the email address provided below.

Any changes will be published on this page.
